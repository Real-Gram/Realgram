RealGram / Shahnameh game

Explore the Shahnameh through stories and quizzes

The Shahnameh is Ferdowsi’s Persian epic, often called the Book of Kings. RealGram brings a game based on this literary world into the same app as your account and messages.

Download RealGram for Android

Learn through a sequence of chapters

Shahnameh in RealGram combines story chapters and quizzes. This offers a way to return to the characters and events in short sessions. It is an introduction to a literary tradition; reading a game summary is not a substitute for studying the full text or comparing scholarly translations.

For Persian readers and curious newcomers

A Persian story game can support conversations about language, mythology and cultural memory. Read a chapter, try its questions and return to details that were unfamiliar. If you are learning Persian, keep a separate list of words and character names to review outside the game.

Start with the Android app

Download RealGram from the homepage and open the Shahnameh section. Use the same REAL-ID when returning so that supported account progress stays associated with your identity. The app’s interface shows the chapters and activities currently available; availability can change with releases.

Progress and REAL

Some game activities connect to the REAL balance in the RealGram ecosystem. Treat in-game balances and any market-traded token as different concepts: completing a quiz is not a promise of income, a cash payout or a future token price. The app’s current rules determine how a particular activity is recorded.
