How it feels, start to finish

Every session runs along one thread.

Not six apps stitched together with a shared login. Connect, play, and earn are one continuous path through the same account — each part picks up exactly where the last left off.

  1. 01

    Connect

    Open the app, and you're already online.

    VLESS + Reality transport and AI-assisted routing carry every message from the first tap — the same engine that keeps RealGram traffic moving on networks that actively fight back. No separate setup, no second app.

  2. 02

    Play & earn

    Step into Shahnameh, a living Persian epic.

    Chapters, quizzes, a tap-to-earn loop with real weight behind it — every session adds toward a balance that's actually yours. Nothing is credited until it's server-verified, not just counted on your phone.

  3. 03

    It's yours everywhere

    REAL lands back in RealGram — same handle, same balance.

    Message, invite a friend, redeem a reward: REAL moves with your identity across the whole ecosystem instead of staying locked inside whichever app happened to earn it.

Freedom · always-on connect

The connection that doesn't wait for permission.

VLESS + Reality and WireGuard tunnels carry every session by default, with AI-assisted routing that keeps adapting mid-session instead of just at connect. Where the ground network is cut off completely, Freedom can fall back to satellite-backed access — the same Starlink-class link the app's own connect screen promises, not a separate product bolted on.

  • No separate VPN app to install or trust
  • Routing that keeps adapting while you're already connected
  • Satellite-backed fallback for when towers and fiber both fail

Shahnameh · the Book of Kings

A thousand-year-old epic, playable.

Ferdowsi wrote the Shahnameh — the Book of Kings — over a thousand years ago to keep the Persian language and its history alive. RealGram turns it into chapters and quizzes told in the epic's own order, so every one you clear is a real piece of that story, not filler between taps.

  • Chapters told in the epic's own order, not shuffled trivia
  • REAL itself was born here, as Shahnameh's own community token
  • Play from the app or straight from a browser tab

REAL · the shared economy

The first Iranian token, born inside a game.

REAL started as Shahnameh's own in-game currency and grew into the balance every part of RealGram now shares — positioned as the first Iranian token approved on blockchain, and the first time a community token was put to work inside a game rather than just traded. It's a jetton on The Open Network (TON); its price is set by the open market, not by RealGram.
