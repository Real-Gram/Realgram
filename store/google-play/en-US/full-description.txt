Realgram brings conversations, voice calls and friends together in one app.

Exchange messages, start voice calls from a conversation, and call back from your recent calls. Keep your messenger profile and share an invitation code to grow your clan of invited friends.

Realgram is free to use and supported by advertising. No subscription or in-app payment is required. Optional rewarded ads can provide bonus connection data.

Connection tools are available separately. Connection data limits apply. Internet access is required.
