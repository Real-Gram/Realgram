# Realgram store material

The en-US and nb-NO folders contain copy ready to paste into the corresponding
Play Console text fields, after confirming it matches the tested release.

See ../../docs/realgram/PLAY_RELEASE.md for the signed AAB workflow and remaining
publication prerequisites. Nothing in this folder has been submitted to Google.

## Assets still requiring the final running app

- 512×512 high-resolution store icon based on the approved Realgram logo.
- 1024×500 feature graphic.
- At least two genuine screenshots of the release build showing the messaging
  and VPN screens without private user data, keys, personal messages or developer controls.
- Additional tablet screenshots only if that device presentation is supported.

Do not use the ad video or the administration screenshots as the app's product
screenshots. Do not fabricate screens of features that are not working.

## Details the account owner must confirm in Console

Existing app/package, developer identity, public support email/contact, privacy
policy URL, account deletion route, target audience/content rating, countries,
Data safety answers and any service declarations. Do not substitute guessed
answers or submit attestations on the basis of this draft.

Test-track release notes, to be finalized against the signed artifact:
Improved advertising diagnostics and banner loading reliability.

Background calling must remain a release gate until verified on two physical phones.
