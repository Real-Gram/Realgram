Meet Hakim AI, the guide inside RealGram

Hakim AI is the assistant feature in the RealGram ecosystem. It gives you a place to ask questions while exploring the app, rather than switching between unrelated product guides.

Download RealGram for Android

Ask about what you are trying to do

Start with the task: finding Shahnameh, understanding a screen or asking what an account-related message means. Include the app version and a short description of the screen if a question concerns a problem. The more concrete the question, the easier it is to check whether the answer actually helps.

Using Persian in your questions

RealGram includes a Persian interface with right-to-left reading support. You can write a question in Persian when using the assistant, while recognizing that the quality and availability of a reply depend on the service behind it. Check technical terms, numbers and names against the relevant screen or published guide.

Check AI answers before acting

An AI response can be incomplete, outdated or wrong. For connection problems, compare advice with the current app settings. For progress or rewards, rely on the account records shown by the service. A conversational answer is not independent evidence that a transaction was completed.

Keep private information out of the prompt

Do not paste account passwords, recovery codes, payment details or VPN connection secrets into a conversation. Describe an error without including credentials. If you share a screenshot for support, cover any sensitive account information before sending it.

Install and find the feature

Download the Android APK through the RealGram homepage, open the app and find Hakim AI in the available features. This page explains the feature’s purpose; it does not promise offline operation, an always-available model or professional advice.

Frequently asked questions

Is Hakim AI part of RealGram?

Yes. It is one of the features presented in the RealGram ecosystem.

Can an AI answer confirm a payment or reward?

No. Check the actual account and transaction records.

Should I share my password to get help?

No. Never include passwords, recovery codes or connection secrets in a prompt.
